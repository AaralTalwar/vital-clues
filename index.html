Vital Clues

The Body Detective. Solve a body mystery by reasoning like a scientist.

Fictional cases for ages 11–15 · nothing about you is collected · not medical advice

How it works

  1. Read the clues and pin them. Each clue goes on the evidence board under a hypothesis — does it support it, or not fit?
  2. Draw your prediction. Before the model runs, you sketch what you think will happen.
  3. Run the body lab. Slide the effort up and watch the body respond. Your sketch sits on top of the model.
  4. Explain it, then transfer it. Write the mechanism in your own words, apply it to a new situation, and see exactly how your reasoning scored.

Pick a case

Four body systems, one way of reasoning. About 8 minutes each.

Every case is fictional. Vital Clues never asks for your name, age or anything about your health.

Before you open the case

Every character in Vital Clues is made up and the numbers are illustrative. This is a science puzzle, not medical advice, and it never diagnoses anyone. If you ever feel unwell, talk to an adult.
